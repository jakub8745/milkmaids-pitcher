The "Milkmaid's pitcher" is an animated sculpture divided by a low poly model of a milk splash. Each fragment inhabits its own "digital reality", currently available in 3D [WebGL] and AR [WebXR].

You can now export a static snapshot of the live sculpture at any moment. Captured models and previews are pinned to Pinata's IPFS service, ready for sharing or minting.

Tip: switch to AR, frame your pitcher, then tap “get a 3D model” to archive that unique pose on IPFS.
